# Design

The shop is reskinned as a Yale candy factory. Routes, catalogue calls, login, stock counts, and the agent's tools are the same.

The night-sky hero, chocolate river, and gold-ticket navigation make the first screen feel like a storefront instead of a form. Wrapper cards, foil hover, and the conveyor slide give the clothes a consistent shelf, including the cards the chat drops on the page. Gumdrop filters still say Hoodies or Crewnecks, and the room name under them tells a shopper where they are.

Each size on an item page keeps its real count and adds a candy-jar fill: plenty, only a few left, or back in the mixing vat. The basket is local to the visit. Tossing a piece does not change the database quantity. One product per visit wears a golden-ticket badge; the code it reveals is a demo, and the price on the card stays the catalogue price.

The chat is the chocolatier: a gold button, a factory-floor greeting, and a stirring indicator. The system prompt uses that voice and still requires tool lookups for price and stock. Account and payment questions are told to drop the whimsy.
